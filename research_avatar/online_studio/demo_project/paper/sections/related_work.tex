\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Noisy intent understanding.} Realistic input variation reduces both intent and slot performance, while aggregate augmentation improves average robustness \citep{sengupta2021robustness}. LAUG expands task-oriented dialogue stress tests to word perturbations, paraphrases, disfluencies, and speech phenomena \citep{liu2021laug}. HINT3 and covariate-shift benchmarks further show that clean in-distribution scores do not establish deployment robustness \citep{arora2020hint3,khosla2022covariate}. These studies motivate shifted evaluation; MTA asks how to allocate a fixed synthetic-noise budget.

\paragraph{Typo robustness and augmentation selection.} Balanced insertion, deletion, substitution, and swap mixtures can transfer from synthetic to naturally occurring noise \citep{karpukhin2019training}, while character processing alone is not automatically robust \citep{belinkov2018synthetic}. Stable encodings, misspelling-aware embeddings, and compact projections attack the representation problem directly \citep{jones2020robust,piktus2019misspelling,sankar2021projection}. Broader augmentation surveys and controlled utterance-classification studies document substantial variation among transformations \citep{chen2023survey,xu2020augmentation}. Decision-boundary sampling selects unlabeled text for annotation \citep{tong2001svm}; uncertainty-guided generation has been studied for efficient active learning \citep{quteineh2020textual}. MTA instead selects labeled synthetic typos under fixed quotas and refit counts. Word-form variation can alter lexical evidence \citep{wang2025word}, motivating model-specific rescoring.
