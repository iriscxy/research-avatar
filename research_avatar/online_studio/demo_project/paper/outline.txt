- Working title: Spend Typo Budget Where the Classifier Is Unsure: Class-Balanced Margin-Targeted Augmentation
- Core claim C1: MTA beats matched class-balanced random selection in every frozen confirmation setting.
- Core claim C2: In the primary setting, MTA remains within one point of full-mixture noisy accuracy with one quarter of its augmented examples.
- Core claim C3: The positive effect spans all four operators and six settings evaluated over the same eight frozen seeds held out from exploration.
- Story: Uniform typo sampling ignores what the current classifier finds difficult. MTA generates the same candidates, ranks them by true-label score gap, and spends equal per-intent quotas on the lowest-gap items. Held-out-seed confirmation identifies a positive matched-budget selector effect while bounding the scope.
- Method: Candidate generation → true-label margin → class-balanced quota selector → shared refit/evaluator → paired bootstrap decision rules.
