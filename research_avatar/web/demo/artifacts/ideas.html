1. Literature Landscape

The literature survey shows a transition from pipeline TOD to end-to-end and LLM agents, while reward learning moves from one scalar signal toward multiple adaptive objectives. The central gaps concern conflict identification, judge validity, public reproducibility, and matched cost.
